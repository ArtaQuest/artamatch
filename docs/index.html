Step 2 · How the matching works

Each tradition makes small, checkable statements: “her Moon is in Rohini”, “his Saturn squares her Moon”, “their year animals clash”. We wrote down 1,293 such statements across eleven traditions — Western sign placements, the couple's Davison chart, the slow outer-planet cycles, the Sun-sign and Moon-sign pair tables, birth nakṣatra and tithi, classical synastry contacts, Chinese day pillars and year animals, and numerology life paths.

Then history set the weights: on 56,621 couples from five centuries of records, a single transparent blend learned how much each statement matters. Nothing else is learned — no black box. Every score you'll see decomposes into named statements.

What the score means: among historical marriages that ended, the chance the ending was a divorce (someone remarried while both lived) rather than a death. It's a ranking from the past — not a prophecy.
