  traditions the 21-module chart sweep (vedic, hellenistic, uranian, harmonics, ...) is ~50,000 raw
             features across its modules — too wide to concatenate into one matrix at this row count.
             It was built and measured separately; under the same control it sits at chance.

The yardstick is WHICH DAY OF THE MONTH the wedding was written down on: 0.5101 controlled.
It carries no astrological content whatsoever. Read every row above against it.

Columns: 'alone' is plain ages plus that family and nothing else. 'add gain' is what it adds to the ages.
'LOFO cost' is what the giant model loses when that family is removed — the honest marginal number, and
far smaller than 'add gain' wherever the families are redundant with each other, which is nearly always.
