How to read this

  • The ranking is the product. The percentages are calibrated to the historical corpus (4.8% of ended marriages ended in divorce by this definition), not to modern rates.
  • Every score is a sum of named statements. Gold chips lower the risk; blue chips raise it. Ask for any candidate's full breakdown — all 1,293 statements are on the books.
  • The era speaks through the slow planets. Neptune and the outer cycle phases carry the largest weights — the birth era read astrologically, which is what an interpolation over five centuries rewards.
  • Your own cohort scores worst. Partners born 1994–1998 rank at the bottom for the fourth model generation in a row.
