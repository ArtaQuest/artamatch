Step 2 · How the matching works

Each tradition makes small, checkable statements: “her Moon is in Rohini”, “his Saturn squares her Moon”, “their year animals clash”. We wrote down 2,749 such statements across the world's traditions — sign placements, the couple's Davison chart, the slow outer-planet cycles, the Sun-sign, Moon-sign and nakṣatra pair tables, synastry contacts, Chinese day pillars and year animals, numerology.

Then history judged them. On 56,621 couples from five centuries of records we ran a non-negative sparse regression — a rule may only add risk, and most rules get thrown out. 120 survived. A pairing's score is simply the sum of the risk rules it trips; the best matches trip the fewest and lightest. Every rule is listed at the bottom of this page, with an example.

What the score means: among historical marriages that ended, the chance the ending was a divorce (someone remarried while both lived) rather than a death. It's a ranking from the past — not a prophecy.
